Your home

What the developer’s specification says is in every house. Check your own handover pack for the exact details.

Heating and air

  • A-rated gas condensing boiler with two heating zones and thermostatic radiator valves
  • Heat recovery ventilation that recovers about 90% of the heat from expelled air; the unit is in the landing hotpress
  • Solar photovoltaic panels, argon-filled low-E windows and high insulation

Services enclosure

  • Boiler, water booster pump and water tank sit in an enclosure outside the house
  • Service work happens outside, so a tank leak won’t flood the house
  • Designed so the energy source can be swapped later without replacing the unit

Fittings and cover

  • Residence 9 windows and Bergerac painted internal doors
  • Sealed gas fires, intruder alarm, Cat 5 wiring and garden lighting wiring
  • Steel garden shed with light and sockets
  • 10-year HomeBond guarantee

Gardens and parking

  • Gardens of roughly 0.27 to 0.35 acres, designed by Cunnane Stratton Reynolds
  • Patio of about 60 m² off the living area
  • Private drive with generous parking

Around Howth

Everything below is walkable or a short ride from the estate.

Walks

  • Tramway Loop Walk, linking to the DART station and the Cliff walks via the Summit
  • Cliff Loop and coastal walks
  • Howth Head Special Protection Area

Getting about

  • Bus routes 6 and H3 stop on Thormanby Road
  • Howth DART station
  • Dublin City Centre and the airport are easily reached

Schools

  • Primary: Scoil Mhuire, St Fintan’s NS, Burrow School, Sutton Park
  • Secondary: St Fintan’s High School (boys), Santa Sabina (girls), Sutton Park

Clubs and pubs

  • Howth Yacht Club and Sutton Dinghy Club
  • Howth, Sutton and Deerpark golf clubs
  • The Summit Inn, a few minutes away
